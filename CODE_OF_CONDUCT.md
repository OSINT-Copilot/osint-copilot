# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

OSINT Copilot contributors pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while addressing the effect of harmful behavior.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never post real investigation material or personal data without a clear,
  lawful reason and consent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, trolling, and
sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report conduct incidents privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/OSINT-Copilot/osint-copilot/security/advisories/new)
and state that the report concerns conduct. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent
possible. Do not use a public issue to report harassment.
