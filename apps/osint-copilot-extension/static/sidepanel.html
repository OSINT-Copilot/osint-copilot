OSINT Copilot

Pair with the app

In OSINT Copilot, open the OSINT settings. Under Browser extension, turn on Accept items from the browser extension, choose Pair new extension and paste the string it shows here. It works once, for five minutes.

The app then asks you to confirm. Nothing else is shared until you do.

Highlights

Select text on the page, then press Highlight selection, or right-click it and choose Highlight in OSINT Copilot. Each highlight and its note are filed in the investigation, on the page's Sources note.

Your highlights show on a page only when you ask, on each visit. Allow this to have them shown again whenever you come back to a page: the extension then looks for your own highlights on every page you open, and sends nothing when it does. Chrome asks for its permission to read the pages you visit, unless page capture already has it.

      Highlights kept in this browser

      This browser keeps a copy of your highlights, for every investigation, so it can show them on pages. Deleting that copy does not touch your investigations, which keep theirs.

      Capture page

      Saves this tab as it is now (an MHTML archive, a screenshot of what is on screen and, for a PDF, the document itself) and files it in the investigation as evidence. Also on the page's right-click menu, and with the keyboard shortcut.

      The first capture asks Chrome for two permissions: to read and change your data on all websites, and to save pages. Chrome words it broadly because a capture can be of any site. The extension uses them only to capture the page you ask it to, when you ask. It does not read pages on its own unless you also allow your highlights to come back on a revisit, under Highlights. If you decline, nothing is captured.
