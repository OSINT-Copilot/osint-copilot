# Operation Northgate: case memo

## Summary
A forum seller, harbor_pharma, sells counterfeit cancer medicines through medsupply-direct.example. The shop names [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Company/Northgate Freight BV|Northgate Freight BV]] as its shipper; customs seized 38 of Northgate's parcels on 20 June 2024. Northgate is 60% owned by [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Company/Brightwater Holdings Ltd|Brightwater Holdings Ltd]] (director [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Person/Marta Koval|Marta Koval]]), itself owned from the Seychelles through a nominee.

## Chain of ownership
[[OSINTCopilot/ftm/ShellCompany/Aurelia Trade LP|Aurelia Trade LP]] → 100% → [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Company/Brightwater Holdings Ltd|Brightwater Holdings Ltd]] → 60% → [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Company/Northgate Freight BV|Northgate Freight BV]]. Aurelia's director, [[OSINTCopilot/ftm/Person/Jonah Ferris|Jonah Ferris]], is a professional nominee (unverified).

## Money
The seller takes BTC and ETH only. A €48,000 payment from Brightwater to Northgate appears on a leaked invoice (one source).

## Shipping
Consolidated at the Waalhaven warehouse and declared as food supplements (Q2 2024 manifest). Buyers collect in Tallinn.
